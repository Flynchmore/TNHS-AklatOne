## Questions before Step 3 and Step 9

- A. Teacher emergency borrowing (no librarian in the library): the teacher times in, requests the book in the app and takes it.
  Should that request count as borrowed at once (due date starts) and the librarian only confirms it later, or stay
  "waiting" until the librarian is back? Students too, or teachers only?
- B. School-issued books: which school year dates (start / end) should the app use, and who confirms the list (librarian
  checks each book, or the member's list is taken as is)?
- C. Lost-book document: is it required before the librarian can close the loan as Missing? Largest file size (suggest 10 MB)?

## Answers to the developer's questions (state on 2026-11-26)

- Real borrowed books and tally: yes. Members shows "N now / M in all"; the phone's My Books counts books lent at the desk too, so both match the server.
- Excel import / export of books: yes, Catalog > Export Excel / Import Excel (template, preview of new / changed / errors, apply).
- Walk-in borrowing: yes, Borrowing > Lend a book (Web/Desktop) and Librarian mode on the phone (scan card + book QR). After Step 3 it is the phone only.
- School-issued books inventory: not yet (Step 9).
- Class Record No.: only students have it (teachers never see it); kept.
- Lost-book document: not yet (Step 9); today the librarian records the return outcome "Missing".
- Visitor's Log offline: Time In / Time Out scans are saved on the computer and sent by themselves when the internet is back. The Desktop's other offline changes wait for the Upload page (automatic upload: Step 3).
- Member activity on the phone: Profile > Activity (recent borrowing) and Statistics; a day / week / month log is Step 7.
- Light and dark: every screen has both themes (contrast-checked); Step 10 re-checks after the changes.
- Member list printout: the only paper with emails, not masked yet (Step 5).
