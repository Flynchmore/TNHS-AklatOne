# Pictures for the welcome slides (member app, first launch)

Put six pictures here, named **welcome-1** to **welcome-6** (PNG or JPG). They are converted to WebP and copied to
`frontend/src/assets/welcome/1.webp` ... `6.webp`; a slide without a picture keeps its drawn illustration.

- Landscape, **4:3**, at least **1200 × 900 px**; keep the main subject in the middle (corners are rounded).
- Bright, clean pictures with little text (the slide already has a title and a sentence).
- Privacy (RA 10173): no student faces unless the school has written consent; hands, backs or blurred faces are fine.
  Do not show real names, Member IDs or LRNs; use the fictional Member ID **JC123456** if a card is visible.

| File | Slide | Idea for the picture |
|---|---|---|
| welcome-1 | Welcome to AklatOne | The TNHS library: shelves and reading tables, or the library entrance with the school seal |
| welcome-2 | Find any book | A hand holding a phone with the AklatOne catalog open, books on a shelf behind |
| welcome-3 | Request, then pick it up | The librarian handing a book across the desk to a student (faces not shown) |
| welcome-4 | Your Library ID card | A printed AklatOne Library ID card (fictional JC123456) next to a book, or being scanned at the desk |
| welcome-5 | Time in at the library | A phone scanning the Visitor's Log QR code on the kiosk screen at the library door |
| welcome-6 | Never miss a due date | A phone showing an AklatOne reminder, with a book and a calendar on a desk |

Drawings or flat illustrations in the school colours (navy #1E326D, blue #3354B5, yellow #F0F000) also work well.
