# AklatOne School Requirements Validation Checklist (Provisional)

Questions for Tagumpay National High School library representatives. These come from the provisional items in `aklatone-library-business-rules.md` and from auth features that were implemented without a recorded decision.

1. How many books may a student or teacher borrow at once? Are limits different by member type?
2. What is the loan period? Does it differ for students and teachers, or by book category?
3. Are there overdue penalties (fines, borrowing suspension)? If so, what are they?
4. Can loans be renewed? How many times?
5. Can members reserve books that are currently borrowed?
6. How are students and teachers identified (LRN, employee ID, school email, username)?
7. May students and teachers create their own accounts (public registration), or must the library provision them?
8. Is "Sign in with Google" allowed? If so, which email domains or accounts?
9. Are one-time codes (OTP) by email acceptable for login and password reset?
10. Which reports does the library need (inventory, circulation, overdue, popular books, others)?
11. What devices and network access do library staff and members have?
12. What data privacy requirements apply (Data Privacy Act of 2012, school policy)?
